## App Description

Turn your iPhone into a fullscreen flip clock and digital desk display for focus, study, and bedside use.

Tiqlo keeps time clear and distraction-free. Place your iPhone on a desk, bedside table, or workspace and enjoy bold digits in an immersive display built for quick, comfortable reading.

Classic Flip Clock

Enjoy the rhythm of changing time. Refined flip animation, subtle depth, and bold digits give every passing minute a satisfying sense of movement.

Multiple Visual Styles

Switch freely between the classic Flip clock and clean Digital displays. Choose from a range of color themes to match your space, mood, or lighting.

Immersive Full Screen

Tiqlo keeps system distractions out of sight. Tap anywhere to reveal the controls; they automatically fade away so the screen can return to what matters most: the time.

Made for Portrait and Landscape

Whether you hold your phone or place it horizontally as a desk clock, Tiqlo automatically adapts its layout and type size for a clear, balanced view.

Designed for the Night

Night Mode lowers the visual intensity and hides nonessential information, making Tiqlo more comfortable to use at your bedside or in low-light spaces.

Time, Your Way

- Choose between 12-hour and 24-hour time
- Show or hide leading zeros
- Optionally display seconds
- Show the date and weekday
- Keep the screen awake
- Save preferences locally on your device

Tiqlo requires no account and has no social feed or complicated navigation.

Less distraction. More focus.  
Let Tiqlo help you see the time—and stay present in it.

4. Tap “More” to access Night Mode, Settings, and About.
